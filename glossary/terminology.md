# Glossary

`Home › Glossary`

**Authority (workflow blueprint field)** — who may decide or act for a given case class and threshold; must resolve to a named role or rule, never "the AI, usually."

**Autonomy ladder** — the six-mode progression (shadow, recommend, assist, approve-to-act, exception-based, bounded autonomy) a workflow climbs as evidence of reliability accumulates.

**Automation bias** — a tendency to accept AI output specifically because it is AI-generated, independent of its actual quality.

**Handback trigger** — the pre-designed condition under which control returns from an agent to a human within a process.

**Human–AI Workflow Blueprint** — the step-by-step design artifact naming AI role, human role, authority, evidence, interface, approval, override, fallback, escalation, and feedback for a workflow.

**Process owner** — the named human or function accountable for a business process's end-to-end outcome; never the agent, regardless of how many steps are agent-executed.

**Progressive autonomy** — the principle that autonomy is granted incrementally and based on evidence, not decided once at launch.

**Rubber-stamping** — approving AI output without genuinely reviewing it; often detectable via fast, uniform approval times.

**Shadow mode** — the autonomy mode where a system observes and produces non-operational output for retrospective comparison, without acting.

**Trust calibration** — designing and measuring for appropriate reliance on a system, neither over-trust nor under-use.
